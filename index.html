How to play

  1. Drag (or tap) a round sound onto a Sprunkee.
  2. Tap a Sprunkee to hush it. Tap again to wake it.
  3. Tap the ✕ to send it home.
  4. Fill all 7 for a SUPER JAM!
  5. Win trophies to unlock new friends and stages.
